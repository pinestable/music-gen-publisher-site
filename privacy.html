Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 October 2026

Music Gen Publisher is a personal tool used only by its owner to upload videos to the owner's own YouTube channels. It has no other users.

Data accessed

When the owner authorizes the app, it receives the owner's Google account email address and OAuth tokens that allow uploading and managing videos on the owner's YouTube channels.

How data is used and stored

The email address is used only to confirm that the authorizing account is the owner. Tokens are stored locally on the owner's computer, are never shared, sold or transmitted to any third party, and are used only to call the YouTube Data API on the owner's behalf.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deletion

Tokens can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and deleting the local token files removes them.
